# FitTribe One-Page Pitch Outline

## Slide Title
FitTribe

## Slide Subtitle
Turn daily fitness check-ins into retention, identity, and revenue.

## Hero Statement
FitTribe is a Kenya-first fitness accountability platform where users log real progress, build streaks, get smarter behavior-based coaching prompts, and earn verifiable rewards without crypto friction.

## Top Right Callout
What exists today:
- Live web app with check-ins, streaks, progress, community, and trainer marketplace
- Gasless Avalanche reward layer with in-app wallet and soulbound achievement badges
- New accountability intelligence: streak risk, dominant habit, weekly summaries, AI refresh

## Four Core Blocks

### 1. Problem
Fitness apps lose users because nothing meaningful happens after day one.

In Kenya, trainers also struggle with fragmented client acquisition and weak recurring engagement.

### 2. Solution
FitTribe makes consistency the product:
- fast check-ins
- streak protection
- community pressure
- trainer access
- rewards tied to real activity

### 3. Why This Wins
- Local accountability loop, not just tracking
- Crypto hidden behind a usable product
- Trainer marketplace plus premium retention layer

### 4. Business Model
- Trainer subscriptions and lead fees
- Premium accountability membership
- Corporate wellness dashboards and challenges

## KPI Row

### KPI 1

### KPI 2

### KPI 3

## Bottom Four Blocks

### Current Focus
Build a stronger check-in loop with behavior intelligence, reminders, streak rescue, and premium accountability before expanding into maps, gyms, or broader commerce.

### Go-To-Market
Start with Nairobi trainers, gym communities, campus streak challenges, and social-led acquisition.

Convert retention into premium and trainer-side revenue.

### Why Now
Mobile behavior, digital payments, and fitness community culture are already here.

FitTribe connects them into one product instead of asking users to adopt crypto first.

### The Ask
Funding to accelerate:
- user growth
- trainer onboarding
- premium accountability
- mobile execution

- This is not a crypto-first app. It is a retention-first fitness product with crypto infrastructure underneath.
