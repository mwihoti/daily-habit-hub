FitTribe | One-Page Pitch

Turn daily fitness check-ins
into retention, identity, and revenue.

FitTribe is a Kenya-first fitness accountability platform where users log real progress, build streaks, get smarter behavior-based coaching prompts, and earn verifiable rewards without crypto friction.

What exists today

  • Live web app with check-ins, streaks, progress, community, and trainer marketplace
  • Gasless Avalanche reward layer with in-app wallet and soulbound achievement badges
  • New accountability intelligence: streak risk, dominant habit, weekly summaries, AI refresh

Problem

Fitness apps lose users because nothing meaningful happens after day one. In Kenya, trainers also struggle with fragmented client acquisition and weak recurring engagement.

Solution

FitTribe makes consistency the product: fast check-ins, streak protection, community pressure, trainer access, and rewards tied to real activity instead of generic motivation.

Why This Wins

  • Local accountability loop, not just tracking
  • Crypto hidden behind a usable product
  • Trainer marketplace plus premium retention layer

Business Model

  • Trainer subscriptions and lead fees
  • Premium accountability membership
  • Corporate wellness dashboards and challenges
Primary wedge Daily habit retention
Monetization first Premium + trainer revenue
Moat Behavior data + on-chain proof

Current Focus

Build a stronger check-in loop with behavior intelligence, reminders, streak rescue, and premium accountability before expanding into maps, gyms, or broader commerce.

Go-To-Market

Start with Nairobi trainers, gym communities, campus streak challenges, and social-led acquisition. Convert retention into premium and trainer-side revenue.

Why Now

Mobile behavior, digital payments, and fitness community culture are already here. FitTribe connects them into one product instead of asking users to adopt crypto first.

The Ask

Funding to accelerate user growth, trainer onboarding, premium accountability, and mobile execution.
